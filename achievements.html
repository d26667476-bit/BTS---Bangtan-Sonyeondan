2023

In 2023, each member stepped into new chapters, yet the seven continued to shine as one in the hearts of ARMY. Every song, memory, and promise you shared became its own kind of treasure. BTS, your music still brings us together, and we will always be grateful to walk beside you.

2024

In 2024, distance could not dim the light BTS had given the world. ARMY held close every memory and every message, sending strength and affection to each member. Your kindness has always been its own lasting legacy, and we are endlessly proud of all seven.

2025

In 2025, seeing all seven members return to one another brought a joy words can barely hold. The care you showed ARMY through every chapter made this reunion even more meaningful. Welcome home, BTS - may the days ahead be full of laughter, music, and the simple happiness of being together.

2026

Through November 2026, BTS has earned international, regional, MTV, and fan-voted group awards, including Global Popular Artist, a KM Chart Daesang for Album of the Year, and multiple honors for “SWIM.” More awards are still to come. See the full 2026 awards list.

Solo Awards
2013

In 2013, seven young dreamers began a journey that would change countless lives. Before individual spotlights, each member already carried a unique spark that made BTS whole. ARMY will always cherish the courage in those first steps and the beautiful story that began with them.

2014

In 2014, the members kept learning, growing, and giving their all side by side. Their different voices and personalities were already precious gifts, each adding a new color to BTS. To every member: you were special long before any trophy could say so.

2015

In 2015, the members individual artistry kept blooming within the group they built together. Every small detail - their voices, humor, sincerity, and care - gave ARMY another reason to love them. Your worth has never depended on a stage or an award; it lives in the hearts you have touched.

2016

In 2016, seven distinct talents met in every performance and made something greater than the sum of its parts. ARMY saw not only artists, but people who kept reaching for their dreams with open hearts. Thank you for letting us witness each members light grow brighter.

2017

In 2017, the world began to notice what ARMY had long known: every member has a light all their own. Yet it is the way those seven lights support one another that makes them unforgettable. We are proud of each individual journey and grateful for the family you remain.

2018

In 2018, each member continued to show that strength can be gentle, creativity can be brave, and sincerity can reach across the world. Your individual colors make BTS more beautiful, never less united. ARMY sees you, values you, and is thankful for the person behind every performance.

2019

In 2019, every member brought a different feeling and truth to the music, giving ARMY comfort in a thousand ways. Some awards are held in hands; others live in the quiet knowledge that your words helped someone keep going. For us, that connection is beyond measure.

2020

In 2020, the members continued to offer comfort and companionship when the world needed it most. Each voice carried its own warmth, and together they made ARMY feel less alone. Thank you for turning difficult days into moments of hope simply by being yourselves.

2021

Selected wins: V - Seoul Music Awards Best OST for Sweet Night

2022

Selected wins: J-Hope - MAMA Awards Most Popular Male Artist; Jimin and Ha Sung-woon - MAMA Awards Best OST for With You

2023

Selected wins: Jimin - MAMA Awards Best Male Artist; Jung Kook - MAMA Awards Best Collaboration for Seven; Jimin - Billboard Music Awards Top Global K-Pop Song for Like Crazy

2024

In 2024, each member followed a different path, but none of them had to walk outside the love of ARMY. We kept every song and message close, proud of the people you are as much as the artists you have become. Seven individual lights, forever cherished.

2025

In 2025, the members came home to a world that had missed them dearly. ARMY joy was not about a prize or a stage - it was seeing each of you safe, smiling, and reunited. May every member feel how deeply you are loved, exactly as you are.

2026

In 2026, every member continues to bring a different kind of magic to BTS and to the world. We celebrate your individual dreams, your shared history, and the kindness that shines through both. No honor could capture what you mean to ARMY; our gratitude is simply here, always.
