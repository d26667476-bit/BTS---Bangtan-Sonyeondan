Early Life & Hometown

Born and raised in the Geumjeong District of Busan, South Korea, Jimin discovered his passion for physical expression and performance at an early age. Growing up, he trained in martial arts, earning a black belt in Taekwondo and practicing Kendo for nearly eight years. As a teenager, he fell in love with dance and entered the prestigious Busan High School of Arts, where he became the top overall student in the modern dance department.

The Audition That Changed Everything

Encouraged by his contemporary dance teacher, Jimin auditioned for Big Hit Entertainment in a private regional casting session in Busan in 2012. His expressive movement and vocal potential helped him stand out, and he was the only dancer to pass that regional audition before moving to Seoul to begin training.

Joining BTS & The Shortest Training Period

Jimin trained for roughly six months to a year, the shortest training period among BTS members. He adapted from contemporary modern dance to fast-paced hip-hop choreography through intense practice, securing his place as the final member added to the group's debut lineup. He officially debuted with BTS on 13 June 2013.

Solo Artistry & Vulnerable Vocal Power

Jimin's distinctive falsetto and emotionally expressive performance style shine in BTS solo tracks such as Lie, Serendipity, and Filter. His solo album FACE (2023) featured Like Crazy, which reached number one on the Billboard Hot 100. He followed it with MUSE (2024), further exploring his identity as a solo artist.
