Early Life & Hometown

Min Yoon-gi was born and raised in the Buk District of Daegu, South Korea. He discovered his passion for hip-hop at a young age after hearing Epik High’s Fly, which inspired him to start writing lyrics by the age of 13. By 17, he was working a part-time job at a recording studio in Daegu to learn music production.

Operating under the underground rap name Gloss, he became an established producer and rapper in the local underground hip-hop scene, composing beats and arranging music even while facing financial hardships and a lack of support for his musical ambitions.

The Audition That Changed Everything

SUGA’s path to Big Hit Entertainment wasn’t a traditional idol audition. In 2010, he saw a flyer for Big Hit’s first “Hit It” rap audition championship. He didn’t audition to be an idol performer; his goal was strictly to join the company as a music producer and songwriter.

He entered the competition and successfully won second place in the final rap battle showcase. His sharp lyricism, unique rhythmic flow, and advanced music production skills immediately caught the attention of company founder Bang Si-hyuk.

Joining BTS & The Producer Trap

After passing the audition in November 2010, SUGA moved from Daegu to Seoul as the second member to join the foundational BTS lineup, right after RM. Famously, Bang Si-hyuk initially convinced SUGA to join the group by promising him that he wouldn’t have to dance much and could focus primarily on producing music. Instead, he was placed into an intensive idol trainee regimen.

To make ends meet during his grueling three-year training period, SUGA worked a part-time job as a delivery driver, during which he suffered a severe shoulder injury that he hid from the company out of fear of being dropped. His perseverance paid off, and he officially debuted as BTS’s lead rapper and co-producer on June 13, 2013.

Solo Artistry & The Alter Ego: Agust D

Beyond his work with BTS, SUGA unleashes a raw, unvarnished underground rap persona through his solo alter ego, Agust D. The name itself is deeply tied to his roots: it spells “DT Suga” backwards, which stands for “Daegu Town Suga.” Under this moniker, he releases raw, self-produced projects that bypass traditional commercial boundaries to address heavy topics like mental health, depression, anxiety, social commentary, and his personal rise from poverty to global fame.

His trilogy of projects—the self-titled mixtape Agust D (2016), D-2 (2020) featuring the global hit Daechwita, and his official studio album D-DAY (2023)—fully solidified his reputation as an elite lyricist, capable of balancing mainstream pop appeal with the aggressive, fast-paced tongue technology of underground hip-hop.
