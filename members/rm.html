Early Life & Hometown

Kim Nam-joon was born in Dongjak District, Seoul, but moved to Ilsan, Goyang, when he was four years old, where he spent his entire childhood. An exceptionally gifted student, he taught himself fluent English by watching the American sitcom Friends and scored in the top 1% of the nation in his high school university entrance exams, boasting an IQ of 148.

He discovered hip-hop in the fifth grade after hearing Epik High’s Fly and began writing lyrics shortly after. Operating under the underground hip-hop name Runch Randa, he quickly built a reputation in the underground rap scene, collaborating with other underground artists and showcasing a lyrical maturity far beyond his years.

The Audition That Changed Everything

RM’s path to global fame began through an underground hip-hop network. In 2009, he auditioned for the independent hip-hop label Big Deal Records, making it past the first round but missing out on the final selection. However, his extraordinary wordplay caught the eye of Korean rapper Sleepy, who was acting as a judge.

Deeply impressed by RM’s potential, Sleepy passed his contact information directly to Bang Si-hyuk, the founder of Big Hit Entertainment. In 2010, RM formally auditioned for Bang Si-hyuk, who was so blown away by the young teenager’s raw poetry and rhythm that he decided to form a hip-hop group entirely centered around him.

Joining BTS & The Foundation of the Group

RM was the very first member to join Big Hit’s new project in 2010, making him the foundational pillar of BTS. The entire group was built around his talent, and the lineup shifted multiple times from a pure hip-hop crew into a mainstream pop-idol group.

For three years, RM trained intensely while acting as the primary anchor for the incoming members, guiding them through the transition to Seoul life. Despite being younger than Jin and SUGA, his incredible maturity, creative vision, and English fluency led him to be appointed the official Leader of BTS, a role he assumed ahead of their official debut on June 13, 2013.

Solo Artistry & The Creative Mind

Beyond his monumental duties as the leader and primary lyricist for BTS, RM has established himself as a highly respected solo artist, poet, and art connoisseur. His solo work reflects deep philosophical introspection, exploring themes of identity, fame, and human connection.

He has released two monumental mixtapes—the aggressive, self-titled RM (2015) and the atmospheric, universally praised mono. (2018). He made his official solo commercial debut with the studio album Indigo (2022), followed by the critically acclaimed, genre-bending experimental album Right Place, Wrong Person (2024), solidifying his rank as one of the most prolific and profound songwriters of his generation.
