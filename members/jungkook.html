Early Life & Hometown

Jeon Jung-kook was born and raised in Mandeok-dong, Busan, South Korea. He enjoyed sports as a child and initially dreamed of becoming a professional badminton player. After seeing a performance by G-Dragon, he became interested in pursuing music and performance.

The Audition That Changed Everything

In 2011, at 13 years old, Jungkook travelled to Daegu to audition for the South Korean talent show Superstar K3. Although he did not advance from the preliminary rounds, industry scouts noticed his potential, and seven entertainment agencies approached him with offers.

Joining BTS & The Golden Choice

Despite offers from several established labels, Jungkook chose Big Hit Entertainment because he was impressed by RM's rapping and wanted to train with him. He moved to Seoul as a trainee and travelled to Los Angeles in 2012 for dance training at Movement Lifestyle. His skills across vocals, dance, and rap earned him the nickname “Golden Maknae”. He debuted with BTS on 13 June 2013.

Solo Artistry & Global Pop Domination

Jungkook's BTS solo tracks include Begin, Euphoria, and My Time. In 2022, he released Dreamers for the FIFA World Cup. His 2023 solo single Seven (featuring Latto) debuted at number one on the Billboard Hot 100, followed by his chart-topping debut solo album GOLDEN.
