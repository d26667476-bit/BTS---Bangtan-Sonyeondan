Early Life & Hometown

Kim Tae-hyung was born in the Seo District of Daegu, South Korea, and spent his formative childhood years being raised by his grandparents in the rural countryside of Geochang County. Growing up in a farming family, he developed a love of music in middle school. With his father's support, he studied saxophone for three years, building musical foundations as he dreamed of becoming a professional performer.

The Audition That Changed Everything

In 2011, V went to a private Big Hit casting session in Daegu strictly to support a friend. A rookie development executive noticed his visuals and charisma and urged him to audition too. After calling his parents for permission, V auditioned on the spot and passed the casting session.

Joining BTS & The “Secret Member”

V signed with Big Hit in September 2011 and moved to Seoul as a trainee. The company kept him out of public pre-debut promotions as a “Secret Member”, then revealed him to the world 11 days before BTS's official debut on 13 June 2013.

Solo Artistry & Jazz Infusions

V's deep baritone and interest in vintage jazz, soul, and R&B shape a distinct solo style. BTS solo tracks such as Stigma, Singularity, and Inner Child showcase his expressive performances. He appeared in the historical drama Hwarang: The Poet Warrior Youth (2016) and released his debut solo studio album, Layover, in 2023, blending low-fi pop with classic jazz arrangements.
