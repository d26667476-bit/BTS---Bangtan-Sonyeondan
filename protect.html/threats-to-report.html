Threats to Report

Report specific safety threats—not ordinary criticism or opinions.

Report content that includes

  • A direct or specific threat to hurt, attack, kill, or abduct a BTS member.
  • A stated plan, time, place, or method for carrying out violence.
  • Claims of bringing or using a weapon, or encouraging someone else to attack.
  • Stalking, following, or attempts to approach a member at a private place or event.
  • Sharing private details—such as a home address, live location, travel plans, or private contact information—with the intent to find, follow, or harm a member.
  • Repeated targeted harassment that includes credible threats or signals a real-world safety risk.

What to do

  • Save the original link, account name, date, and a screenshot if it is safe to do so.
  • Report it to HYBE through the portal instructions and use the platform’s report feature.
  • Do not repost the threat, contact the person, or try to investigate them yourself.
  • Do not share private information further. If personal details are exposed, report the post promptly.

Is someone in immediate danger? Contact local emergency services or police now. Give them the original evidence and location details you have. Do not wait for an online report to be reviewed.

Not every negative post is a safety threat

Disagreement, criticism, jokes, or an unpleasant opinion by themselves are not the same as a threat of violence. Report content based on what it actually says or does, and do not make false or mass reports.
