HYBE Portal Instructions

Use this official portal to report harmful posts or other violations affecting HYBE artists.

HYBE’s portal may be displayed in Korean. If you need help understanding it, use your browser’s translate feature and check the form details before submitting.

Open HYBE Artist Protection Portal ↗

How to make a report

  1. Open the official portal using the button above.
  2. Choose the report option that best matches what happened and complete the form.
  3. Include the member’s name and a short, factual description of the threat or harmful activity.
  4. Copy the original post or page link. Add the account name, platform, and when you saw it, if available.
  5. Attach clear screenshots or other evidence if the form allows it. Keep the original files unchanged.
  6. Submit the report once. Save any confirmation or reference number the portal provides.

Keep your report useful and safe

  • Report the original content; do not repost it, quote it, or spread it to get attention.
  • Do not argue with or threaten the person who posted it. Do not organize a pile-on.
  • Share only the information the form asks for. Never publish your own private details or someone else’s.
  • Stick to what you saw. Do not guess who posted it or add claims you cannot verify.
  • For urgent or credible danger, contact local emergency services or police as well. A web form is not an emergency service.
