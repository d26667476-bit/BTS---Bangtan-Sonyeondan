Remembering Zahra

Zahra was an ARMY from Iran who loved BTS, especially Taehyung. She faced a difficult heart condition, and her words carried so much love and courage.

For Zahra

Zahra, your love reached toward the music and the person who brought you comfort. Your words are full of tenderness, and your place in this purple universe will be remembered with care.

Thank you for sharing a piece of your heart with BTS and with ARMY. May your memory be held gently, with all the warmth and kindness you deserve.

Until we meet in another purple universe, Zahra. Rest gently, surrounded by love.
