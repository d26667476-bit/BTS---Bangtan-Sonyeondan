Racial discrimination and BTS

Public statements against anti-Asian discrimination and racial injustice

BTS speaks out against anti-Asian hate

In March 2021, amid a rise in anti-Asian violence, BTS shared a statement describing discrimination they had experienced as Asians. They said they had been subjected to insults, mocked for their appearance, and asked why Asians spoke English. They expressed condolences to victims and their families and said: “We stand against racial discrimination. We condemn violence.”

The statement spoke about their own experiences while emphasizing that these were “inconsequential compared to the events” affecting others. It was a public expression of solidarity, not a claim that the group’s experiences were identical to those of every person affected by racism.

A separate act of solidarity: Black Lives Matter

In June 2020, BTS and their agency, Big Hit Entertainment, donated $1 million to Black Lives Matter. In a later statement reported by CNN, BTS said they stood against racial discrimination and violence. The donation and the 2021 anti-Asian-hate statement concern different communities and events; they are related through the group’s public opposition to racism, but should not be conflated.

Keeping the context clear

Discussion of BTS and race can also include criticism of cultural appropriation in some early styling or performances. That is a distinct subject from the anti-Asian discrimination described in BTS’s 2021 statement. This entry focuses on the statement shown in the supplied image and the group’s documented public actions against racial injustice.
