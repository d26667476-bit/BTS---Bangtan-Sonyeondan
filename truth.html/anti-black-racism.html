Anti-Black racism accusations

Early-career criticism, BTS’s public statement, and the 2020 donation

What the criticism concerns

Criticism of BTS’s early career has included concerns that some styling and performances borrowed from Black American hip-hop culture without enough attention to its history, and that some language or performance choices were insensitive. Other specific claims circulate online, but details and translations can vary. Each allegation should be checked against its original recording or reliable reporting rather than treated as established solely because it is repeated.

This entry recognizes that the criticism exists without claiming to settle every disputed incident or infer intent. Appreciation for hip-hop and later support for racial justice do not, on their own, resolve questions about cultural appropriation or past conduct.

BTS’s June 4, 2020 statement

In response to racial injustice and the Black Lives Matter movement, BTS posted a statement in Korean and English on its official account:

“우리는 인종차별에 반대합니다. 우리는 폭력에 반대합니다. 나, 당신, 우리 모두는 존중받을 권리가 있습니다. 함께 하겠습니다.”

“We stand against racial discrimination. We condemn violence. You, I and we all have the right to be respected. We will stand together. #BlackLivesMatter”

English text posted by BTS on June 4, 2020. See the original post and contemporary Variety coverage.

Donation and fan fundraiser

In June 2020, BTS and Big Hit Entertainment donated $1 million to Black Lives Matter. Black Lives Matter confirmed the donation to Variety. The BTS ARMY subsequently matched the amount: Variety reported that the fan-led campaign raised $1,026,531 from 35,609 donors within 24 hours.

These are documented acts of support for Black Lives Matter. They are relevant context, but they should not be treated as proof that all criticism of earlier conduct was answered or that every concern was resolved.

How to read the response

The June 2020 statement was a public expression against racial discrimination, and the donation was a material contribution to Black Lives Matter. The cited reports do not describe either action as a specific apology for every earlier accusation. Keeping those distinctions clear allows the documented support and the earlier criticism to be considered without conflating them.
